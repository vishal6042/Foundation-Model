11 limitations

L1 and L2

L1

Closed label set (R1)

Simple words. It learns patterns alone, but can only name an activity it was shown labelled examples of.

Breaks on: “How many times did someone vacuum today?” No public dataset labels vacuuming.

What goes wrong. No output for vacuuming. Adding it needs days of labelled examples a family will not provide.

HomeFM fix. Minutes are trained to sit near sentences (SigLIP). A new concept is a new sentence, no labels.

Status: tagger designed; alignment built per window

L2

Binary events only (R2, R6)

Simple words. Meters (power, temperature, humidity) are squashed to on/off and the amount is thrown away.

Breaks on: “Is my fridge OK?” A failing compressor runs 25 min instead of 12 and draws 20 % more.

What goes wrong. Both look like fridge ON … OFF. No energy answers, slow faults invisible, hand-tuned thresholds.

HomeFM fix. Numbers enter as numbers (sent when the value changes), normalised per device.

Status: numbers as input built; detectors designed
