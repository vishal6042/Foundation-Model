Architecture · step 2

Moment encoder: any number of events → one vector per minute

Why fixed minutes. A cooking minute has 40 events, a night minute none. One vector per minute makes “the last 4 hours” always 240 steps, in busy and quiet homes alike.

Why not just average. 38 motion events would drown the one stove turning on, the most important thing that happened.

Four learned reporters (K = 4 latent queries, Perceiver cross-attention, segment softmax for all minutes at once) each learn to focus on different things.

Quiet key. An empty minute still gets a proper vector: “nobody moved all morning” is information.

Added: the event count (log scale) and the minute's time of day and weekday.
