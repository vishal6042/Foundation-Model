11 limitations

L7 and L8

L7

Forecast without timing (R7)

Simple words. Guesses which events are likely soon, but not when, or in what order.

Breaks on: “Will Dad be up soon?” Bedroom events are likely in the next 30: in 5 min or 3 h?

What goes wrong. No useful when-forecasts; skipped meals or missed medicine cannot be detected.

HomeFM fix. The next-event head predicts device, value and time until it happens (log-normal mixture).

Status: next-event head with Δt built

L8

Not searchable by text (R10)

Simple words. Its vectors are not connected to language: you cannot type a question and find moments.

Breaks on: “When did someone come home late this week?”

What goes wrong. Open-ended and summary questions cannot use the model at all.

HomeFM fix. Minute vectors are projected into the text space; the agent's semantic search finds them.

Status: alignment built per window; vector store designed
