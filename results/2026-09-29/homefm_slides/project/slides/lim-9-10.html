11 limitations

L9 and L10

L9

No audio or vision (R2)

Simple words. It cannot hear or see: anything that flips no sensor is invisible.

Breaks on: “How many times did my kid cry?” “How many parcels came yesterday?”

What goes wrong. Whole domains (childcare, pets, deliveries) cannot be answered.

HomeFM fix. On-hub detectors send tags with a confidence as Home Tokens; HomeFM fuses them. Raw media stays home.

Status: detectors designed

L10

Small, old pretraining data

Simple words. Learned from a few public datasets: mostly one-person homes with older binary sensors.

Breaks on: A new home with Matter plugs, a smart lock, a doorbell camera and a robot vacuum.

What goes wrong. Understanding of new devices rests only on their text description, with no practice data.

HomeFM fix. More homes, energy datasets, simulated homes with injected faults, pilot and donated homes.

Status: 84 public homes in use; simulator scaffold; pilots planned
