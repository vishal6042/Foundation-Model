DomusFM, the baseline

DomusFM in one slide (Fiori et al., arXiv 2602.01910)

Event
time · sensor · ON/OFF

Sensor as text
item, type, room → MiniLM (frozen) + status + cyclic time

Attribute self-attention
→ one event vector

12-layer transformer
over the last 30 events, d = 384

Heads
activity (linear) · next-k bag of events

Pretraining, no labels

Two contrastive phases (InfoNCE): hide one attribute of some events, then hide whole events with the event layers frozen.

Fine-tuning

A small head trained on 5–30 % of the target dataset's human-made activity labels. Leave-one-dataset-out over 7 public datasets.

Edge-sized

36M parameters, under 500 MB, ~10 ms per window on a Celeron mini-PC. Beats DeepCASAS, Chronos and a GPT-2 baseline.

Our reimplementation: 28.6M parameters (the paper leaves some sizes unspecified), in src/homefm/baselines/domusfm/.

DomusFM is the primary baseline. It is a strong idea: describing sensors in words is what makes cross-home transfer work.
