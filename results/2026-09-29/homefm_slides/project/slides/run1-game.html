DomusFM, the baseline

Pretraining is a game: find your own window

Before it sees any labels, the model studies millions of unlabelled events by playing this game over and over.

1

Take a window of 30 events

18:29:04 fridge door OPEN
18:29:40 fridge door CLOSED
18:30:12 stove ON
… 30 events in all

2

Make a copy and hide some of it

18:29:04 fridge door ???
18:29:40 ???
18:30:12 stove ON
…

3

Find the copy among 128

Mix the hidden copy with 127 other windows. The model must pick out its own copy. The other 127 are the wrong answers.

Why play it

No labels are needed. To win honestly, the model has to learn how homes behave, for example that the stove usually comes on after the fridge.

The two numbers

30 events = one window (from the paper). 128 windows = one training round, called a batch (our choice: the paper does not say).

The catch

Like a photo quiz where every photo shows its date: you can match dates without looking at faces, score 100 % and learn nothing. That is what happened in run 1.

Technical terms: the game is contrastive pretraining with the InfoNCE loss; the 127 other windows of the batch are 'in-batch negatives' (paper §4.3). Phase 1 hides single facts of 15 % of events (attribute masking); phase 2 hides 15 % of whole events (event masking). Window of 30 events: paper §6.2.2. Batch size 128: not reported in the paper, our choice (configs/domusfm.yaml).
