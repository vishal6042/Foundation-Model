11 limitations

L5 and L6

L5

One label per window (R5)

Simple words. One label per moment; never where an activity starts and ends, and no overlaps.

Breaks on: “How many times did we cook today?” A 2-minute pause splits one session into three.

What goes wrong. Counts and durations unreliable; cooking while the baby cries loses one of the two.

HomeFM fix. Several tags per minute, a start/end head, per-activity rules (merge gaps, minimum duration).

Status: designed

L6

No anomaly or device health (R6)

Simple words. Describes what happens, but not how unusual it is, or that a device is broken.

Breaks on: “Anything unusual last night?” Front door at 03:12; a PIR stuck ON looks like someone home.

What goes wrong. No security alerts, no fault detection, no early warning of changing routines.

HomeFM fix. Next-event surprise, −log p(event | history), plus anomaly and device-health engines that explain flags.

Status: surprise score built; engines designed
