Each device's own timeline over time: a health vector per device, compared with its past and the fleet.

Built: fusion, moment encoder, stream transformer (both modes), event read-out, next-event head. HomeFM is encoder-only; the only decoder is the LLM agent, outside the model.

Encoder-only: every transformer turns inputs into vectors. No text is produced on the model path.
