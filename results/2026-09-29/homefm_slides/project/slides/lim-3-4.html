L3 and L4

L3

Fixed 30-event window (R3)

Simple words. Always the last 30 events: about 2 min in a busy kitchen, 6 h at night, never weeks.

Breaks on: “Cooking in the last 4 hours?” A 40-min session spans many windows; none sees start and end.

What goes wrong. Long activities cut up, quiet periods blurred together, slow trends invisible.

HomeFM fix. Fixed 1-minute slots (4 h = 240 steps), a stream of hours, day tokens for weeks.

Status: 1-minute stream built; day tokens designed

L4

Single occupant (R4)

Simple words. Assumes one resident. A motion sensor fires the same for grandma, her grandson or the dog.

Breaks on: “Did grandma eat lunch?” while her grandson cooks at noon.

What goes wrong. No people counts, no per-person care questions, pets cause false alarms at night.

HomeFM fix. A person card per resident and pet, per-room counts; honest ranges with motion sensors only.

Status: designed
