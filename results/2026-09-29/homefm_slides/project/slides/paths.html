Architecture

Three answer paths, and an honest refusal

Fast path

The concept is stored (cooking, parcel, bark): exact SQL over events, episodes and states.

Open path

Not stored: the question becomes a vector and is matched against stored minute vectors, with a confidence.

Analysis path

Anomaly, device health, comparison with baselines, forecasts.

Capability check

No sensor can observe it: an honest “I can't sense that here”.

Promotion loop. An open-path concept that is asked often is added to the stored concept list, and from then on it answers by the fast path. Because minute vectors are kept, its full history is filled in retroactively.
