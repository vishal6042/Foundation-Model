Pretraining · game 1

Game 1: predict the next event, and when

L_next = CE(device) + CE(state) + λ·L_value + NLL_LogNormalMixture(Δt)

Which device: scored against every row of this home's registry, so it never predicts a device the home lacks.

When: a log-normal mixture over the wait, so “in 10 s” and “in 3 h” can both be likely.

Surprise for free: how wrong a guess was, −log p(event | history), is the anomaly signal.

Cannot be cheated: the future is unseen, with no copy or timestamp to peek at. Every event is a question: dozens per window.

Weak spots: past only, and short-sighted (much of it is “motion OFF after motion ON”).
