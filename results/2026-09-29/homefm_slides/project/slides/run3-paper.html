Pretraining helps at least as much as in the paper: Kasteren A +0.33 at 30 % labels (paper +0.11), MuRAL +0.19 (paper +0.04), UCI B +0.30 (paper +0.24).

Lower absolute scores come from the test, not the model: with random folds, which let near-copies of test windows into training (likely the paper's protocol), Kasteren A reaches 0.82 and MuRAL 0.79 at 30 % (paper: 0.68 and 0.80).

Caveats: Kasteren C looks high because 83 % of events are “go to bed” (even untrained: 0.86). Next-30 gains on these small sets are near zero (paper: +0.15 to +0.25).
