Biggest gain: UCI B model

Activity +0.20 / +0.30 (5 % / 30 % labels); Kasteren A close behind with the single biggest gain, +0.33. Why: the two smallest test sets (4,666 and 2,636 events): from scratch, so few labels give only 0.26–0.28, so pretrained features fill the gap.

Most consistent: CASAS model

Helped 23 of 24 settings on 6 homes, and the only model that helps next-30 on every home it was tested on (+0.01 to +0.07). Why: its test homes look like its pretraining data (CASAS style sensors, about 95 % of batches), so it also learned their event order.

Smallest gain: Kasteren C model

+0.011 on average. Why: no room to improve. 83 % of its events are “go to bed”, so even the untrained model scores 0.85. Its top absolute score (0.88) says little about the model.

Gains are averaged over activity and next-30 at 5 % and 30 % labels (4 settings per home; the CASAS model covers 6 homes, 24 settings). Pretrained models are near-identical, so 'best model' means where pretraining paid off most. Source: results/2026-09-29/domusfm_corpus_clean/results.json.
