From model to answers

Anomaly, device health and occupancy

Anomaly engine

score = w1·surprise + w2·rarity + w3·drift

Surprise: −log p(event | history) from game 1.

Rarity: how far the window vector is from this home's history (nearest neighbours).

Drift: episode timing, duration and frequency against a per-person baseline.

Every flag is explained: “front door opened at 03:12; normally no door events after 23:00”.

Device-health engine

Sensor faults: silence, stuck-on, battery decay, radio signal drop, event rate that disagrees with neighbouring sensors.

Appliance faults: power-signature vectors against the device's own history and the same type in other homes: longer fridge compressor cycles, changed wash cycles, standby creep.

Occupancy and identity

People per room, supervised by camera or radar counts where allowed; person cards for who.

Motion-only homes get a calibrated range (“1–2 people”), not a falsely confident number.
