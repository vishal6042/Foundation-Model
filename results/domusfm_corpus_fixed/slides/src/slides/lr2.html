DomusFM reproduction · 18 · Source: docs/LIMITATIONS_AND_REMEDIES.md

DomusFM turns every sensor into ON/OFF events and remembers the last 30 events, however long they took. HomeFM keeps real values, reads in 1-minute steps, and adds daily summaries for long-range habits.
