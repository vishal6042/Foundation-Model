HomeFM's remedy for L11

Three harder practice games

Game 1

What happens next, and when?

Predict the next event and the time until it. The future is unseen, so the model must learn routines. Also gives the surprise score (L6) and timing (L7).

Game 2

Hide a big chunk, guess its meaning

Hide 10 minutes, a room or a device type, and predict what it meant. No ON/OFF partner or timestamp is left to copy.

Game 3

Match minutes with sentences

Pair minutes with descriptions, allowing many right answers, so two nights of sleep end up together (L1, L8).

Built and tested on small data. Next: HomeFM against the fixed DomusFM on the same 77 homes. The DomusFM "fill in the blanks" change is a small version of game 2.
